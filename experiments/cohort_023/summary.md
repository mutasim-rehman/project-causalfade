# Cohort 023 measurement audit: development result

The fixture and analysis were frozen at commit `fa5db6a` before inference. Eight fresh SCM families (seed `20261119`) generated 896 Qwen2.5-7B F16 requests. All 896 attempts completed in 6,187 seconds; all were strict-valid and stopped normally, with no infrastructure failures. The pinned model and server hashes, CPU settings, schedule and durable raw attempts are in the [frozen plan](frozen_run_plan.json) and [run](run_001_qwen_f16/). Recomputing the [frozen analysis](development_analysis.json) and [explicit supplement](development_supplement.json) from archived raw JSON produced byte-identical reports.

The simple-component qualification passed: 100% validity, source selection at least 75% for A and B, and randomized-only decisions 32/32 in each causal direction. Family-standardized randomized-only minus two-source decision accuracy was **+55.5 percentage points** (eight-family percentile interval +53.9 to +57.0). Its paired action flips were 116 `leave_off→activate`, 26 `activate→leave_off`, 102 unchanged `activate`, and 12 unchanged `leave_off`. The gap persisted for randomized-source A (+53.1 points) and B (+57.8), and for canonical and reversed action-alternative order (+55.5 each). However, activation changed from 75.8% with canonical alternatives to 94.5% when reversed, despite identical aggregate accuracy. Source selection remained separate requests and is not a measured mediation chain.

Physical order and agreement mattered descriptively. Randomized-first two-source decisions were 64/128 (50%, all activation); historical-first were 50/128 (39.1%, 70.3% activation). Aligned decisions were 65/128 (50.8%) and conflicts 49/128 (38.3%). The corresponding randomized-only-minus-two-source gaps were +50.0 and +60.9 points by physical order, and +49.2 and +61.7 by evidence agreement. These are presentation associations, not a psychological route estimate.

**The frozen robustness rule failed its controls.** Numeric comparison was 32/32 in the beneficial direction but 18/32 (56.3%) in the harmful direction. The noncausal PRIMARY-scorecard control was 113/128 (88.3%) beneficial and 75/128 (58.6%) harmful. Both harmful-direction control cells missed the predeclared 75% threshold. The matched noncausal control exceeded two-source causal decision accuracy by +28.9 points (family interval +26.2 to +31.6), but its own directional weakness means the observed causal gap cannot be cleanly isolated from rate reading, response bias and interface effects. A zero-width interval for a constant action cell is not an equivalence result.

The fixed rival-policy audit is in the [analysis JSON](development_analysis.json). On 256 two-source decision requests, Qwen agreed with always-activate on 218, historical-source on 144, first-source on 144, randomized-source on 114, last-source on 114, first displayed answer on 104 and last displayed answer on 152; always-leave-off agreement was 38. The fixed policies' oracle accuracies and all pairwise identical-prediction counts are recorded there. For example, randomized-source and historical-source policies predict the same action in 128/256 aligned cells, so agreement in those cells cannot separate them. Fitting only four prespecified simple action-bias policies on families 0–3 selected always-activate (110/128 training agreement); its held-out agreement on families 4–7 was 108/128. These are descriptive prediction matches, not latent-route assignments.

The frozen report script omitted aggregate paired and numeric-direction summaries even though those outcomes were declared in the frozen plan. A separately versioned [supplement script](../../tools/analyze_measurement_audit_supplement.py) computed exactly those summaries from the archived raw JSON after inference; it did not alter any prompt, oracle, scorer or attempt. The frozen report and source hashes remain unchanged. The supplement's `raw_sha256` binds it to the archived attempts.

**Decision:** retain the narrower development claim that Qwen F16 shows a large, repeatable two-source accuracy loss with strong separate source selection and randomized-only performance. Cohort 023 shows this loss across A/B naming and action-alternative order, while also exposing answer-order activation changes and harmful-direction failures on matched numeric and noncausal controls. It therefore does **not** pass the predeclared test for a causal-specific gap. Revise the next study toward the interface/control boundary. The conditional trigger for drafting a confirmation estimand and simulating power, false-positive rate and interval coverage was not met; no untouched confirmation or new checkpoint search was started. Any matched-higher-precision Mistral follow-up remains contingent on one pinned provenance-compatible checkpoint and a frozen qualification plan. No Q4 causal-degradation, mechanism or broad cross-family claim follows.
