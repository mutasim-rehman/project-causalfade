## Study and interpretation rules

Eight fresh SCM families each yielded 1,472 prompts per arm. The three fixed lineages were Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3 and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. Each had F16, Q8_0, Q6_K, Q5_K_M, Q4_K_M, Q3_K_M and Q2_K variants. Within a lineage, every format received the same task IDs and schedule. The fixture crosses true causal direction, alignment of historical association, physical source order, randomized-source A/B name, displayed answer order, and semantic versus `X=0`/`X=1` response contracts for decisions and matched noncausal scorecards. It also contains governing-source selection, randomized-only decisions and single-rate numerical checks.

The frozen plan classifies this as a **development measurement screen**. It defines no cohort-024 confirmatory primary endpoint or multiplicity-adjusted test. The archived analysis supplies eight-family percentile bootstrap intervals for descriptive paired effects. A post-collection diagnostic reuses cohort 023's 75% component/control and 95% validity thresholds; those checks must not be described as cohort-024 preregistered pass/fail gates. Each response is not an independent experimental family, and three fixed checkpoints do not support a population claim about all language models.

## Higher-precision references

- **Qwen F16:** 1,472/1,472 strict-valid; governing-source selection 242/256; randomized-only decisions 64/64 under each response contract. Two-source decisions were 111/256 semantic and 144/256 neutral. The semantic randomized-only minus two-source gap is 56.6 percentage points. Harmful-direction semantic decisions were 7/128. Harmful numerical comparison was 18/32 and harmful matched noncausal scorecards were 75/128 semantic and 58/128 neutral. Thus strong source identification and isolated decisions coexist with a two-source deficit, but matched controls do not isolate that deficit as causal-specific.
- **Mistral F16:** 1,472/1,472 valid; governing-source selection 128/256. The model selected the **first displayed alternative in all 256 selection prompts**: A when A was listed first and B after reversal. Randomized-only decisions were 38/64 semantic and 37/64 neutral. Two-source decisions were 134/256 semantic and 137/256 neutral. Its low source-selection and isolated-decision competence disqualifies a clean source-use dissociation comparison.
- **Llama F16:** 1,464/1,472 valid; governing-source selection 188/256; randomized-only decisions 64/64 under each contract. Two-source decisions were 151/256 semantic and 152/256 neutral. Harmful noncausal scorecards were 71/128 semantic and 81/128 neutral; eight semantic controls ended at the 64-token limit. Source selection and matched control performance prevent a causal-specific interpretation.

None of the three F16 references passes all post-collection diagnostic component and control checks. All controls are included in the scheduled denominator; no model or task cells were removed to improve the result.

## Within-lineage precision patterns

**Qwen:** F16, Q8_0 and Q4_K_M semantic two-source decision scores were 111/256, 109/256 and 110/256. The Q4-minus-F16 family-paired change was −0.4 percentage points (descriptive 95% family bootstrap interval −1.6 to +0.8). Q3_K_M and Q2_K scored 150/256 and 149/256, but harmful matched controls deteriorated and Q2 governing-source selection fell to 136/256; Q2 selected the first displayed source alternative on 96.9% of valid source prompts. These are changed response policies, not demonstrated improvement in causal reasoning. Neutral-contract scores vary separately (F16 144/256, Q4 128/256, Q3 155/256, Q2 128/256).

**Mistral:** Source selection stayed at 128/256 from F16 through Q3_K_M, following the first displayed alternative. Semantic two-source accuracy stayed near one-half (F16 134/256; Q4 130/256; Q3 129/256). At Q2_K, validity fell to 1,343/1,472, with 118 invalid semantic two-source responses and nine invalid semantic randomized-only responses. Of 129 invalids, 113 stopped at the 64-token limit and 16 stopped with noncompliant content. Its semantic two-source score of 71/256 is dominated by output failure and cannot be read as selective causal damage.

**Llama:** F16, Q8_0 and Q4_K_M semantic two-source decisions were 151/256, 151/256 and 150/256. Q4_K_M produced 448/1,472 invalid responses: 256/256 semantic noncausal scorecards, 191/256 neutral noncausal scorecards and one semantic randomized-only item. Every one ended for length at the 64-token cap. Consequently its strict semantic noncausal score was 0/256, while its semantic causal score stayed 150/256. The enormous apparent Q4 causal-minus-control interaction reflects a response-format and length-limit boundary in the control task; it is not evidence of improved causal reasoning. Q3_K_M again had 1,469 valid responses and 225/256 semantic noncausal correct, underscoring that the precision response is nonmonotone.

The full analysis reports all seven levels per lineage, with per-direction F16-paired effects and causal-minus-matched-control precision interactions. Those many contrasts are exploratory. Their percentile intervals describe resampling of **eight** families; they do not correct for selecting a favorable contrast among 18 quantized arms and multiple tasks.

## Psychology-adapted response measurement

For each arm, task type and response contract, the archive reports activation hits on beneficial items, activation false alarms on harmful items, their **valid-response denominators**, and descriptive signal-detection `d_prime` and criterion coordinates using the stated half-count boundary correction. Invalid responses remain wrong in scheduled accuracy and are not silently mapped to `leave_off` or `X=0`. These coordinates depend on an unverified equal-variance latent-evidence model, so they are response summaries rather than identified psychological processes.

Qwen F16 semantic causal decisions had 104/128 beneficial hits and 121/128 harmful false alarms (`d_prime` −0.69), showing a strong activation tendency despite near-perfect isolated decisions. Its semantic matched noncausal items had 118/128 hits and 53/128 false alarms (`d_prime` 1.61). The task contrast helps localize the problem to two-source causal wording or reasoning, but instruction semantics also differ and remain a live alternative. For Llama Q4_K_M semantic noncausal items, zero valid responses make `d_prime` and criterion undefined; the archive records `null` rather than imputing a boundary value.

## What the data support

The matrix demonstrates that aggregate intervention accuracy is sensitive to source naming, answer position, response contract, activation preference, checkpoint and quantization format. In Mistral, crossing displayed alternatives reveals a first-option source-selection rule that the fixed A/B prompt in cohort 022 could not diagnose. In Qwen, the isolated-evidence versus two-source gap repeats, but weak harmful controls remain. Llama Q4 shows a large, format-specific strict-output failure under the fixed 64-token budget. These are **behavioral and measurement findings** under the frozen prompts, not internal-process or general causal-capability estimates.

No F16 reference supplies the full component/control competence required for a clean causal-specific quantization claim. No monotone precision trend appears across the three fixed lineages. The published F16 GGUFs for Mistral and Llama are third-party conversions with source revisions and hashes recorded; the publishers' original conversion commands were not independently verified. This limits claims that cross-lineage differences arise solely from architecture.

## Decision and next study

Treat cohort 024 as a completed development matrix. Do not pool its 30,912 responses as independent trials or use its strongest exploratory interval as a confirmatory result. Choose between two bounded paths before more inference: (1) validate a response contract and matched control on fresh task forms, then freeze one causal-specific precision estimand; or (2) develop a measurement paper on how source selection, answer position and format distort intervention-choice benchmarks, with a prespecified transfer test. Either path needs untouched SCM families, a primary endpoint, cluster-level power/coverage checks and independent task forms. Preserve all failed controls and invalid outputs in the release.
