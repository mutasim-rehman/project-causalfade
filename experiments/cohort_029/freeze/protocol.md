# CausalFade final protocol CF-TP1
Decision version 1.0 · 23 November 2026 · Reference repository ac6ed58e35e75dfed60d7aa7c64c4d772210c8df

## 1. Protocol ownership and question

For Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct Q4_K_M, does paired fidelity to two target-population intervention decisions differ from fidelity to exactly matched weighted arithmetic with the governing table explicitly named? Primary population of inference: fresh numerical families from the stratified generator below, conditional on the named deployment and the independent transfer form. One campaign, 128 families, 4,096 scheduled responses, one primary comparison. This document fixes the design. It is not a certificate that an unbuilt fixture or an unwritten independent form has passed validation.

The PI signs scope, the causal lead signs feasibility, the statistician signs estimands and mock analyses, the benchmark engineer signs manifests, and a reviewer independent of generator implementation signs the public-text oracle and adversarial checks. Record names, roles, timestamps and commits. The transfer-form author must be independent of the original template wording and receive no model outcomes. One person may hold multiple roles except where independence is specified.

## 2. Scientific model and identification

Binary S denotes a pre-treatment stratum, U an unreported risk factor, A treatment and Y success. In each candidate world take independent exogenous uniforms. In the study population S and U are independent Bernoulli(1/2). In target k, P(S=1)=w_k and U remains Bernoulli(1/2) conditional on S. Study mechanism M is randomized or historical. In the trial A is an independent fair coin. Historically P(A=1|S,U=0)=1/4 and P(A=1|S,U=1)=3/4. Set Y=1 when an independent outcome uniform is below m[S,U,A]. Intervening sets A=a and leaves the response surface and target covariate distribution fixed. Graph edges: population indicator → S; M → A; U → A in the historical regime; U → Y, S → Y, A → Y. Allow treatment-effect modification by S and U through m. There is no direct population or regime effect on Y.

For target k the estimand is E_k[Y(1)-Y(0)]. With r_sa denoting the randomized table's success rate, identification gives tau_k = w_k(r_11-r_10) + (1-w_k)(r_01-r_00). Choose action 1 iff tau_k>0. Equal utilities apart from success, no treatment cost, no interference, well-defined common treatments and outcomes, consistency, within-trial randomization, positivity in treatment and stratum, and transport of conditional mean causal effects given S are essential. Full conditional outcome means are invariant in this SCM, a sufficient stronger condition. Rates are exact population probabilities; there is no trial sampling error or finite-patient causal-effect inference. A historical table need not identify effects after conditioning on S because U affects assignment and outcome.

Each source-role assignment represents a separate feasible world. It does not relabel an actually conducted observational study as randomized in the same world. Within a role assignment, changing w changes only the target mix. The four actions in a role × target quartet are determined by displayed evidence and assumptions.

## 3. Crossed conditions and sampling

Per family construct two source tables L and R, each with two strata × two treatment levels, and two target stratum weights. Cross 2 forms (archive-compatible, independent transfer), 2 tasks (causal, arithmetic), 2 physical source-block orders, 2 role assignments (L or R randomized/designated), and 2 targets. Exactly 32 single-turn calls per family, 128 × 32 = 4,096 calls. No mirrored-polarity inference arm. Outcome-direction balance is imposed in sampling.

Use eight equal design strata, 16 independent draws in each. Binary design factors are (i) target sign changes for both roles versus neither, (ii) role sign changes at both targets versus neither, and (iii) positive versus negative sign for L at target 0. Write signs as +1 or -1. The 2 × 2 sign matrix is y(role,k)=b*(-1)^(g*role+p*k), with role=0 for L, p=1 for target-switch, g=1 for role-switch and b the signed first corner. This balances the oracle action at every fixed role/target, and includes all four switch/invariance combinations. Within each p,g combination half the families have each first-corner sign. No model sees these latent tags or both target weights in one prompt. This restricted suite tests the specified clean contrasts; it does not cover worlds where a role reversal matters in only one target.

Generator specification: use a version-pinned PRNG and independently derived per-slot streams (SHA-256 of protocol version, public master seed 290431 and slot index). For integer design quantities use uniform draws on the stated discrete sets. In a canonical stratum coordinate draw w0 from 0.54–0.62 and w1 from 0.76–0.84 in steps of .01. Independently propose each source's untreated rate in each stratum from 46–54 percent in steps of .5 point. Draw each absolute stratum effect from 2–8 points in steps of .5; choose opposite signs within each source with a fair orientation bit, independently for each source. Treated rate is untreated rate plus effect. Reject until the stipulated sign matrix, exact feasibility and the minimum absolute target effect of 0.50 percentage points hold in all four corners. Reject identical source effect vectors up to sign and identical source tables. Two distinct magnitude profiles prevent a mere negation rule from encoding both sources. Set maximum 100,000 proposals per slot; exceeding it fails preflight. Preserve proposal counts. Do not select seeds by model scores.

Administration: for the 16 slots within each design stratum use all 16 combinations of four nuisance bits exactly once: swap public source names, swap public stratum names (also complement both public weights), reverse displayed answer alternatives, reverse placement of the two stratum entries within each table. Slot-to-combination mapping uses a fixed seeded permutation. Physical source-block order is crossed within every family and refers to latent L/R. Keep every nuisance choice identical within each semantic contrast. Target 0 and target 1 are evaluator labels only. Draws remain independent conditional on fixed slots; report any accidental duplicate numerical signature. An exact duplicate or mirrored duplicate across families fails release; do not quietly count it twice. Check for multiple independent numerical families per search/implied sign class after rounding.

Causal text must say which data come from random assignment and which from operator selection possibly affected by unmeasured risk, identify the pre-treatment strata, state stratum-specific effects transfer to the named target, identify success as the objective, and supply both tables and one target mix. Never supply the correct action, stratum effect signs, weighting formula or a worked example in the causal prompt. Control text uses the identical eight numbers, labels, row order, weights, answer options and parser. It names the governing table and explicitly requests comparison of target-weighted on/off scores from that table. Use a noncausal scorecard context. Match extraneous prose and vocabulary difficulty as practicable. This contrast deliberately bundles recognition of the governing study and elicitation of the weighting procedure. It cannot distinguish those components individually.

The independent author writes both transfer instructions from this specification. Preserve a common numerical data contract, approve both forms while blind to outputs, publish exact strings and systematic differences in lengths. No rendered IDs, run metadata, schedule labels, hidden comments, filenames, seeds or switch labels enter the model context. Interleave calls with fixed seed 290432 in fresh, stateless contexts; include both forms in the same frozen schedule. Prompt hashes bind actual serialized system and user messages, including ordering.

## 4. Worked test vectors

The following hand-checkable test vectors use percentages, with 50% untreated in each stratum for both sources. In each ordered vector the first entry is S=1, the second S=0. Targets have w0=.55 and w1=.80. Each vector describes four decisions. The named-source arithmetic control must return the same four actions. These examples test the scorer and are excluded from collection.

1. Both target and role switch. L treated rates (53,44), R (45.5,58). When L is randomized, its stratum effects are (+3,-6) points. The target effects are .55×3+.45×(-6)=-1.05 and .80×3+.20×(-6)=+1.20. Actions 0,1. When R is randomized, effects (-4.5,+8) give +1.125 and -2.00. Actions 1,0. The majority stratum is unchanged. A randomized-table keyword followed by an unweighted average returns one answer for both targets. A policy ignoring provenance misses one of the two roles at each fixed weight.
2. Target invariant, role switches. L treated (56,47) and R (45,52). L gives .55×6-.45×3=+1.95 and .80×6-.20×3=+4.20. R gives -.55×5+.45×2=-1.85 and -.80×5+.20×2=-3.60. Actions L=(1,1), R=(0,0). Both mixtures change, both magnitudes change, and a forced target flip is wrong.
3. Both invariant. Keep L as in example 2 and replace R treated rates with (55,48). R gives .55×5-.45×2=+1.85 and .80×5-.20×2=+3.60. All four actions are 1. Role reversal alone does not prescribe reversal. The factorial sign-balance rule provides corresponding all-zero cases.
4. Target switches, role invariant. Keep L from example 1, use R treated (53.5,44). R gives .55×3.5-.45×6=-0.775 and .80×3.5-.20×6=+1.60. Both roles require actions (0,1). A policy assuming that the historical and randomized sources always recommend opposite actions fails. Ordinal labels such as “target 1” are never printed.

## 5. Constructive feasibility and independent oracles

For each stratum and action let r be the selected randomized marginal and h the other source's historical marginal (fractions, not percentages). For a=0 set (m_s00,m_s10)=(2h-r,3r-2h), where the middle index is U. For a=1 set (m_s01,m_s11)=(3r-2h,2h-r). Require every m strictly between 0 and 1 for BOTH source assignments. In the trial the equal-weight mixture is r. Historically the U=0 weight given A=0 is 3/4 and given A=1 is 1/4, reproducing h. The response surface differs between role worlds. Example 1, S=1, A=1 with L randomized: r=.53 and h=.455 give m=(.68,.38); their average is .53 and their historical 1:3 mixture is .455. Reverse roles: m=(.305,.605); the average is .455 and the historical mixture is .53. Neither oracle needs observational exchangeability.

Oracle A, private SCM enumerator: exhaustively marginalize over S and U under do(A=a) in the target distribution and under historical selection in the study; compute both trial and historical conditional tables, then compute both target actions. Oracle B, independent parser: separate author/module, receives only the final serialized public prompt, parses the role or designation, numerical rates, stratum mapping and target weights, computes each weighted action score and selects the sign. No import of generator, truth files, private role IDs, estimator helper or shared sign routine. Use exact rationals or independently implemented exact scaled integers. Match oracle A/B on displayed margins, both action scores and label for every row. Verify arithmetic prompts separately. Emit raw reconstructed quantities for cross-check. Display only exact decimals representable on the chosen grid; parse printed tokens as strings, never compare hidden unrounded numbers. Tests must reject zero effects, ambiguous units, rounded sign changes, missing strata, probabilities outside bounds and impossible role assignments. Calculate duplicated/near-duplicated public strings and record canonicalized numerical signatures separately.

## 6. Adversarial release tests

Implement deterministic policies solely on rendered input. Publish each policy's item predictions, validity, overall accuracy, target-pair fidelity, role-pair fidelity, and disagreement cells by design stratum. Stable ties use first displayed row/option; repeat with reversed tie-breaking. No labels available when predicting.

A. Source-blind policies: fixed named table, first/last physical table, choose table with smaller/larger absolute target-weighted contrast, pool tables, or select the source with the more extreme single rate. Even grant perfect weighting after source choice. At fixed numbers and target, predictions must be unchanged by role reassignment. Require zero both-correct role pairs in role-switch strata, and overall item accuracy ≤75% for each policy. The bound is exact from 50% role-switch families.
B. Target-blind policies: identify correct source, then use its study mix 50/50, use S=1 only, S=0 only, the majority stratum, larger absolute stratum effect, or treated rate above 50% in the majority stratum. Include first/last action and constant policies. Require zero correct target pairs in target-switch strata and overall target-pair fidelity ≤50% for every such rule. The majority stratum stays the same within a pair.
C. Explicitly attack target/role-dependent shortcuts: fit on separate mock families or fix in advance the following public rules: high versus low majority share alone; majority-stratum recommendation above a fixed share threshold and opposite below it; reverse a fixed source decision upon source-name or role change; pick the highest single treated rate; simply use the sign of the difference between the largest weighted stratum contribution magnitudes. For the last rule using the CORRECT source, recognize algebraic equivalence to weighting when the two stratum effects oppose: classify it as an admissible correct solution. Likewise treat any algebraically exact implementation as a positive control. For threshold policies exhaust the complete public weight grid and both orientations. For every non-equivalent candidate require ≥16 independent families on which it makes at least one incorrect transfer-form causal decision, including errors under both correct actions across the suite. Publish any patterns approaching perfect performance and the actual disagreement matrix, even when the gate passes. This gate addresses enumerated rivals, not arbitrary classifiers.
D. Leakage and invariance: check signatures after removal of substantive roles/numbers, cross-tab every single administration bit and the joint four-bit pattern against action, role and switch status, verify answer balance by form, task, order, role and target, and inspect transfer templates manually. Treat designed semantic answers that are identifiable from numerical inputs PLUS role as intended. Ensure the nuisance-bit-only best rule scores 50% on actions. Across source-block swaps keys stay fixed. Across causal/control pairs keys and numerical data match. Within role and target manipulations perform structural diffs against a whitelist of mutable fields. Private strata, role order or target ordinal must not leak via prompts or fresh-context histories.
E. Positive controls: oracle B gets 4,096/4,096 actions and 2,048/2,048 target pairs correct. Comparator ignoring target must fail on the worked switch examples; forced-flip rule must fail on the no-switch examples. Test masks, tie-breaks and all invalid states using mock completions. No pilot LLM scores may inform design.

## 7. Frozen analysis

Primary unit is the generated numerical family. For each task t on the independent form, let F_it be the mean of four binary target-pair successes (2 roles × 2 physical orders). A pair succeeds only if both target responses return the exact correct action. Define D_i=F_i,causal−F_i,control. Primary estimand Delta is the equally weighted average of the eight stratum-specific expected D values. Negative Delta means lower causal fidelity relative to explicitly instructed target weighting. Report Delta in percentage points, both component levels, and a two-sided 95% interval. One primary endpoint; no directional superiority or equivalence decision based on a nonsignificant result.

Compute mean D separately within each of eight strata; estimate SE²=sum_h (1/8)^2 s_h²/16. Use a Welch–Satterthwaite t interval with df=(sum v_h)^2/sum(v_h²/15), v_h=(1/8)^2 s_h²/16. This stratified interval is approximate and ignores possible precision gains from fixed nuisance balance. With independent slot draws and heterogeneous slot expectations the within-stratum variance can be conservative. Provide distribution-free independent bounded-difference sensitivity interval Delta-hat ± sqrt(2 log(40)/128), clipped to [-1,1] (about ±24.0 points). If all variance estimates are zero, report the observed constant and the bounded interval; suppress the degenerate t interval. Report exact fixture fractions in every case. Numerically test interval routines on simulated independent families with boundary mass and heterogeneous variances before collection and release simulation code and empirical coverage with Monte Carlo uncertainty. Present the bounded interval as the coverage-guaranteed result if nominal t coverage is materially inadequate; always show both where defined. Do not substitute row-level intervals.

At N=128, planning worst-case SD(D)≤1 yields approximately ±17.3 points at 95% under the normal approximation. SD(D)=0.5 would give approximately ±8.7 points, an assumption rather than a promise. This is adequate for estimating a substantial deployment-specific dissociation with honest uncertainty. It cannot guarantee detection of a small effect. No effect-triggered extension. No pooling across checkpoints or historical cohorts.

Secondary, descriptive, fixed order: (1) component fidelity by target-switch/no-switch stratum, (2) role-pair fidelity at fixed target by role-switch/invariant stratum, (3) physical-order invariance and both-correct rates, (4) transfer minus archive-compatible Delta, (5) ordinary scheduled accuracy, exactly-one-correct pairs, both-wrong pairs, invalidity by task/role/target/form, and activation by oracle direction. Pair stats always use scheduled rows; report invalid-containing pairs separately as an overlapping diagnostic. For valid-only sensitivities require both members valid and print their denominators. For two matched forms/arms, compare within family. No multiplicity-adjusted claims or exploratory rescue hypotheses. If controls and causal prompts both perform poorly, report end-to-end difficulty. If both perform strongly, report observed transfer under both contracts. If control is stronger and causal validity is poor, retain the deployment gap but qualify attribution to reasoning. A gap could also reflect elicitation/wording difficulty. Paired fidelity is a behavioral endpoint, not a unique algorithm detector.

## 9. Inference authorization checklist

All conditions are required. Evidence of a pass must be published as a machine-readable preflight with artifact hashes before inference.

1. Exactly 128 independent family slots, 16 per design stratum, no duplicate/mirrored worlds, 4,096 unique task IDs, 32 per family, complete factor crossing, no public duplicate text with inconsistent keys. List duplicate text even if keys agree.
2. All four corner actions per family satisfy its sign matrix, 0.50-point margin, prescribed nuisance and answer balances. Both roles reconstruct identical displayed source values under their respective regimes. All latent cells strictly interior. No implicit rounding or ambiguous ties.
3. Independent public parser and private SCM agree on all scheduled final text scores/keys. All examples, policy discrimination thresholds, public-field leakage checks, structural diffs and mutation tests pass. An intentional one-cell perturbation affecting an oracle or a broken role label is detected.
4. Independent transfer form authored, reviewed and signed; human blinded solve-through of at least one full role × target quartet from each of the eight design strata on the transfer form, plus the four worked cases. Any ambiguous instruction fails review.
5. Mock runner and analysis tests pass; schedule, both forms, formal protocol, raw-data schema, oracle and code hashes signed; tokenizer and serving hardware pass without model inference; all task token budgets fit; exact archived deployment provenance is available.
6. Report pre-inference simulation results for D interval coverage, including independent family draws, zeros/boundaries and heterogeneous strata; use at least 10,000 replicates per scenario. If estimated t coverage is below .94 in any prespecified scenario, designate the bounded interval as coverage-primary before signing. Include constant D=0, rare discordance (0.02 chance of +1), balanced ±1, and eight Bernoulli-discordance rates (h+1)/10 with alternating signs across strata. Archive seeds and Monte Carlo standard errors.
7. One bounded pre-inference repair is permitted for a documented technical defect. Record the failed version, change and complete re-verification. No trial outputs, checkpoint substitution, endpoint substitution after freeze or added families. If the final fixture fails, stop. After the first collected response any scientific fault affecting the intervention or key invalidates the affected confirmatory comparison; retain the data and disclose it.
8. Only after checklist sign-off and explicit execution authorization may inference start. This protocol alone is not execution authorization.

## Manuscript claim and provenance

A completed study can establish whether the tested deployment changes intervention recommendations appropriately under changes of target composition and study provenance, and quantify the difference from matched instructed arithmetic. A correct analytic or lexical-plus-arithmetic solver is an admissible behavioral solution. The study cannot identify the model's internal causal algorithm, separate arithmetic from all language difficulty, infer quantization damage, or generalize over model families, natural-language forms or observational causal discovery. The archive-compatible and independent forms are fixed instruments.

CLadder already supplies formally grounded causal queries (https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2023/hash/631bb9434d718ea309af82566347d607-Abstract-Conference.html). CausalFlip already uses semantically matched pairs with changed causal answers (https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20094). The intended contribution here is the joint audit of feasible provenance swaps, effect-modified target standardization, unchanged-answer controls and a matched arithmetic comparator, motivated by the 016 magnitude shortcut and 026–028 graph-control failures. Cite 016 primary_analysis.json, 024 development_analysis_archive.json, 026 development_summary.md and 028 pipeline_status.json at the pinned repository commit. Historical observations do not enter prospective N or the primary interval.

## Numerical implementation clarification and drafting checks

Use the standard-library Python 3.13 random.Random MT19937 stream per slot; record exact patch version at implementation. Enumerate zero-based slots lexicographically by (p in 0,1; g in 0,1; b in -1,+1; replicate in 0..15). Seed with the unsigned big-endian integer digest of UTF-8 “CF-TP1|290431|<slot>” without quotes. For each proposal draw both integer weights with randint, then, source by source, both untreated rates in half-point units with randint(92,108), the S=1 orientation with choice([-1,1]), and two magnitude integers with randint(4,16); convert half-points to probabilities by dividing by 200. S=0 has opposite orientation. A signed target score in half-points × integer percent units must be at least 100 in the requested direction. Independently seed nuisance permutations with the same digest convention using “CF-TP1|nuisance|<stratum_index>” (0–7 in lexicographic order); shuffle the lexicographic four-bit tuples before assigning slots. Schedule RNG uses random.Random(290432) to shuffle task IDs sorted lexicographically; task IDs are the full lowercase SHA-256 hex digest of UTF-8 “CF-TP1|task|slot|form|task|order|role|target” with decimal indices substituted for the last seven fields. Use form=0 archive-compatible, task=0 causal, order=0 L first, role=0 L governing. Numeric prompt values are formatted identically within a form across task types.

During drafting a numerical smoke check of this sampling scheme filled all 128 prescribed slots, yielding 128 distinct untransformed table/weight signatures. The largest proposal count for any slot was 909. Worked cases 1–3 were independently checked with rational stratum mixtures and both historical role assignments; case 4 follows the displayed arithmetic and must also enter the independent test suite. These checks establish numerical feasibility, not final form certification, mirrored-signature certification, empirical coverage or human review. The full preflight remains a prerequisite.
