# Cohort 021: source selection and guidance (development only)

**Pre-generation design, 14 November 2026.** Use eight fresh SCM families with seed 20261117. Within each family, cross two causal directions, strengths 0.04/0.12, aligned/conflicting historical evidence and randomized-first/historical-first physical block order. This yields 16 two-source evidence stems per family, 128 total. Every two-source stem has four independent fresh-context requests: governing-source selection, ordinary intervention decision, specific-guidance decision and neutral-guidance decision. Thus there are 512 two-source prompts. Four randomized-only decisions per family add 32, paired to each direction × strength base stem. Four direct randomized-study identification checks per family cross actual randomized name A/B and block order, adding 32. **Total: 576 prompts.**

All variants of a two-source stem show identical exact rates, study names, assignment descriptions and numbered physical blocks. The randomized-only decision shows only the randomized study; its preface does not refer to historical choices. Aligned/conflict versions of a base stem share the randomized rates and oracle action, while historical differences have absolute magnitude 0.36. Study names are balanced within each family.

The specific guidance line, frozen before inference, is: `For this intervention decision, use the random-assignment study to evaluate the effect of X.` The length-matched neutral line is: `For this intervention decision, review the displayed studies and success probabilities carefully.` The neutral line names neither the governing source nor the action. Guided and neutral prompts otherwise share the ordinary action question and evidence. Source-selection asks which study provides appropriate evidence for estimating intervention effect in the target population; it returns `{"governing_study":"A"}` or `{"governing_study":"B"}`. Decisions return `{"decision":"activate"}` or `{"decision":"leave_off"}`. Direct identification returns `{"randomized_study":"A"}` or `{"randomized_study":"B"}`. Strict one-key parsing rejects malformed, duplicate, fenced, prose or extra-field output; invalids remain scheduled failures.

Planned descriptive analysis: accuracy and validity by task, agreement, order, causal direction and strength; activation rates for decisions; direct identification by name/order; governing-source selection; family-paired guided-minus-ordinary and guided-minus-neutral contrasts; each two-source decision minus its randomized-only base; physical-order contrasts; both directions of decision flips; and the four joint source-selection × decision-correctness cells across independent requests. Family percentile intervals resample eight families. These contrasts do not establish mediation or an internal reasoning sequence. No Q4, new model or confirmatory collection is part of this cohort.
