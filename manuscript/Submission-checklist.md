This is still a demanding venue for a one-deployment proof-of-concept study. The article uses the two-column IEEEtran journal class and IEEE numbered bibliography style. Check the [current TNNLS Information for Authors](https://doi.org/10.1109/tnnls.2026.3723918) and the [IEEE journal submission guidance](https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/submit-your-article-for-peer-review/the-ieee-article-submission-process/) at filing.
