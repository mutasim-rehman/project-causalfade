\section{Identification and oracle construction}
The binary variables are pretreatment stratum $S$, latent risk $U$, treatment $A$ and success $Y$. Independent uniform errors generate trial assignment $A=\mathbf1\{E_A<1/2\}$, historical assignment $A=\mathbf1\{E_A<(1+2U)/4\}$, and $Y(a)=\mathbf1\{E_Y<m_{SUa}\}$. In the study, $S$ and $U$ are independent fair Bernoulli variables. The target changes the prevalence of $S$ while preserving $P(U=1\mid S)=1/2$. The exact value of intervening is
\[
V_k(a)=\sum_{s=0}^1P_k(S=s)\sum_{u=0}^1\tfrac12 m_{sua}.
\]
Bayes' rule gives $P(U=1\mid A=1,S=s,M=\mathrm{historical})=3/4$ and $P(U=1\mid A=0,S=s,M=\mathrm{historical})=1/4$. For a fixed stratum and action, write $x=m_{s0a}$ and $y=m_{s1a}$. Then $x+y=2r$, where $r$ is the displayed randomized probability. When $a=0$, $3x+y=4h$; when $a=1$, $x+3y=4h$, with $h$ historical. Solving yields the two surfaces given in the main text. Interior-cell checks for each role assignment guarantee feasibility. A sign margin and exact display precision exclude ambiguous decisions.

For family 096 the treated success rates in its first stratum are $r=.540$ and $h=.500$ when L governs. Latent treated probabilities for risks zero and one are $.620$ and $.460$. Their average is $.540$ and their historical $1:3$ mixture is $.500$. With R governing, $r=.500$ and $h=.540$ give $.420$ and $.580$. The historical mixture is then $.540$. These worlds share printed numerical panels. Holding one world fixed while changing target gives weighted contrasts of $-0.570$ and $+1.555$ percentage points (pp) in the first world, and $+1.070$ and $-1.055$ pp in the second. A prompt exposes one assignment and one mixture.

\section{Statistical procedure}
For stratum $h$, family $i$, task $q$ and its four transfer pairs $j$, define $F_{hiq}=\sum_j\mathbf1\{\mathrm{both\ correct}\}/4$ and $D_{hi}=F_{hi,C}-F_{hi,A}$. If $s_h^2$ is the sample variance of the sixteen differences within stratum,
\[
 \widehat{\Delta}=\tfrac18\sum_h\bar D_h,\quad
 v_h=\frac{s_h^2}{8^2\cdot16},\quad
 SE=\sqrt{\sum_hv_h},\quad
 \nu=\frac{(\sum_hv_h)^2}{\sum_hv_h^2/15}.
\]
The approximate 95\% interval uses $t_{.975,\nu}SE$. Independent slot draws with $D_{hi}\in[-1,1]$ give the distribution-free radius $\sqrt{2\log(40)/128}$, clipping endpoints to $[-1,1]$. If estimated variance is zero, the protocol suppresses the degenerate $t$ interval. Pre-inference calibration at seed 290433 used 10,000 replicates per scenario. Reporting-rule coverage was 1.0000 for constant differences, .9986 for rare discordance, .9539 for balanced extremes and .9521 for heterogeneous strata. In the rare scenario 745 replicates used the bounded fallback. These simulations supported the frozen approximate-$t$ designation within the scenarios examined.

Figure 3 marginal intervals apply the same stratified variance calculation separately to $F_C$ and $F_A$. They give 23.55--26.45\% and 24.17--32.86\%. These are descriptive, manuscript-stage calculations. The primary inference uses paired differences. Roles, orders and forms within a family add no independent units. The parameter equally weights the eight design strata and conditions on one deployment and fixed transfer wording.

\paragraph{Deterministic policies and robustness.}
Source-blind policies use a fixed name, first or last table, smaller or larger absolute standardized contrast, pooled rates or an extreme cell. Target-blind policies use the governing source with equal weights, a single stratum, the majority stratum, the largest stratum effect, an extreme rate, option position or a constant action. Other public rules threshold the majority weight, optionally reverse a majority-stratum recommendation, or invert a fixed source decision conditional on the governing name. All grid thresholds and endpoints are tested with both orientations and equality conventions. The positive control compares magnitudes of opposing, weighted stratum contributions, algebraically equivalent to the correct sign here. Unconditional role and target flips each fail on 1,024 invariant pairs. Designed balance limits a nuisance-bit-only rule to 50\% item accuracy.

A constant-action rule cannot make errors under both gold actions. The frozen clarification required eligible constants to get half the items correct, err in at least sixteen transfer-causal families and solve zero required target reversals. Twelve literal failures across the two tie conventions remain recorded. The clarified gate had zero failures. Only declared constants and mathematically constant endpoint thresholds qualify for this exception. The attached \path{data/policies.csv} supplies every audited variant under each tie convention, with total correct items, correct pairs by axis and switch status, and transfer-causal families with errors. \section{Development history and inferential status}
The project began with a precision-by-conflict hypothesis. Crossing causal and associative recommendation directions makes action preferences visible in aligned and conflicting cells. A CNI-inspired tree additionally attributes choices to successive causal, associative and residual preference routes. Distinct policies can have the same observable action probabilities, and the homogeneous tree imposes fit restrictions. The 005 F16 additivity diagnostic violated such a restriction. Accordingly, no psychological-route parameter is interpreted as a validated property of an LLM. Process dissociation motivated crossing compatible and incompatible cues. Signal detection theory motivated distinguishing directional response tendency from discrimination. Two strength levels in the 018 pilot cannot identify a general psychometric curve or a two-dimensional causal/associative evidence surface.

Graph switching addresses another form of observational ambiguity. Confounder and mediator structures can share the full observational distribution while requiring different intervention decisions. An additional irrelevant graph change supplies an invariant control. In 026--028, reference systems failed qualification on the joint decision and control criteria. The later precision arms therefore produced no evidence. CF-TP1 uses an identifiable decision from supplied study designs while retaining switch and invariance contrasts. The register includes the nested 025.1 audit. Earlier fresh-family replications are identified individually, with no pooling into either final study.
