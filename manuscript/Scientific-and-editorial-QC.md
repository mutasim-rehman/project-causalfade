# Scientific and editorial review

**Disposition.** The scientific package is suitable for author review. Its strongest claim is that a mathematically checked resource distinguishes correct reactions to changed inputs from action preferences, incorrect reversals and changes where the key stays fixed. The prospective study estimates an uncertain difference between two end-to-end tasks. The package contains a substantive correction to one part of the tagged narrative.

## Adversarial reading and resolution

1. **Could the impressive historical order result be obtained while ignoring provenance?** Yes. Reading just the two printed differences and choosing the sign of the smaller magnitude reproduces all 768 Cohort 016 keys. This was reconstructed from the released prompt text and checked against the oracle. The 96-family order contrast remains prospective. The heuristic audit is retrospective. The paper identifies the different evidential roles where the two findings are reported.
2. **Does a quarter of target pairs correct demonstrate useful target sensitivity?** A constant action gets 25% fidelity on this design by solving half the invariant pairs and no switch pairs. In the observed causal condition, the same aggregate includes 125 correct invariant pairs and three correct reversals. Figure 4 and the corresponding matrix retain direction and oracle status. Equality to the constant score does not identify an identical policy.
3. **Could control performance or sampling error explain a difference?** The arithmetic form itself solved only 146/512 target pairs. The sole prespecified contrast was −3.515625 pp, with approximate 95% interval −7.7713 to +0.7400 pp and conservative interval −27.5237 to +20.4924 pp. Family-level analysis and both intervals are included. There is no equivalence test, precision manipulation, or independently identified component-specific effect in 029.
4. **Are provenance switches mathematically possible?** The linear mixture solution constructs two distinct response surfaces for the two role assignments. Private enumeration and separate public-text parsing agree for all messages. Family 096 is a collected numerical family whose recorded weights and probabilities yield all four displayed values exactly. Within a fixed world, changing the target changes mixture weights alone.
5. **Could the count of activations be reconciled with directional accuracy?** The tagged prose could not. Re-parsing every response envelope and joining to the frozen gold action gives transfer-causal off/on correct counts of 14/512 and 497/512. Transfer-arithmetic counts are 231/512 and 316/512, with 597/1,024 activations. The machine-readable secondary row cells independently yield those totals. The discrepancy is localized in the supplement and tracked in the ledger. Maintainer acknowledgement or an appended correction referencing the immutable tag would help readers follow the difference. The original data and tagged files were preserved.
6. **Were all gates met before outcomes were seen?** The journal binds the original freeze. Twelve literal policy failures are disclosed together with the frozen constant-rival clarification. The signed human materials recovered later do not certify the intended pre-inference independence gate. The manuscript describes the result as a prospective fixed-form case study with that qualification. An implementation cross-check and an independent human review are different evidence.
7. **What is genuinely new?** The combined audit of feasible role swaps, target standardization, direction-correct switch and invariant pairs, matched arithmetic and enumerated rivals is demonstrated on a completed deployment. Literature comparisons credit prior causal oracles, contrastive evaluation, evidence-cue manipulations, context transfer and standardization. The review uses primary records, including labeled preprints for newer work. Its scope is a targeted methodological review, without a systematic-review coverage claim.
8. **Does the evidence transfer beyond the experiment?** One quantized checkpoint, two fixed English forms, constrained sign patterns and low arithmetic fidelity narrow inference. The transfer causal instruction is longer than its control. Manipulating the causal text changes both source selection and elicitation of arithmetic. A multisystem ranking, causal-specific quantization effect and inferred internal algorithm are outside the evidence.
